Questions

Frequently asked

Which devices can you protect?

Virtually any smartphone, and many tablets and smartwatches. Because we cut film to size on-site from a large device library, we're not limited to what's in stock.

How long does it take?

Most cut-and-install visits take under ten minutes. The film itself is cut in roughly two minutes.

What does the display warranty cover?

On Ultra Premium Care and Premium Care, the 1-year warranty covers accidental external and internal cracks. Liquid damage is not covered. Terms, eligibility and claim limits apply.

How does the 60/40 authorised service option work?

For eligible display replacements at authorised service centres, Trevix365 pays 60% of the eligible cost and you pay 40%. It's subject to plan, model, eligibility and a maximum claim limit.

Clear, matte or privacy — what's the difference?

Clear keeps maximum clarity, matte reduces glare and fingerprints, and privacy narrows viewing angles so people beside you can't read your screen.
